🚀 Codomax Module 4 — RAG, Embeddings & Vector Databases

I built a “Chat With Your Documents” RAG prototype with Python, Streamlit and Gemini! 📚

The app extracts text from PDFs/TXT files, splits it into chunks, retrieves relevant passages using TF-IDF vectors and cosine similarity, and asks Gemini to answer with document references.

What I learned: LLM hallucinations, retrieval-augmented generation, document processing, chunking, similarity search, API integration and error handling.

This prototype uses TF-IDF retrieval rather than neural embeddings or a persistent vector database; integrating those is my next improvement.
